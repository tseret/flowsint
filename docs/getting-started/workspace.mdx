# Investigation workspace

Open a case to record questions, comments, and findings in **Investigation
workspace**. Questions can be assigned to existing collaborators. Findings keep
supporting evidence references and an analyst assessment; owners and admins can
accept or reject them. Changing a finding or its evidence resets its review.
The activity feed records the actor and changes, including collaborator and
analysis edits. Existing investigation roles govern all access.

Select an entity or relationship to attach a comment, question, or finding to
that particular part of the graph. Collaboration items and activity are stored
on the server. They are not evidence-file uploads: attach source references or
observation descriptions in the supporting-evidence field.

## Enrichment results

**Recent enrichment runs** distinguishes results, no matches, partial results,
missing credentials, quota limits, and failed runs. Expand it for input/output
counts, elapsed time, and errors. The enricher launcher shows required credential
names and recent run availability without revealing stored secret values.
Provider errors are not evidence of no matches.

Relationship details show the provider, run, retrieval time, source observation
time (when supplied), and source reference. Repeated runs retain multiple
observations. Retrieval time and observation time describe different events:
retrieving historical DNS today does not make that resolution current.

VirusTotal passive DNS follows up to five pages by default. `max_pages` accepts
1–25 pages; truncation is reported as a partial result. Domain/IP reputation
lookups use existing provider data. TLS results create certificate entities.
Ports identify endpoints by host, number, and protocol.

## Graph views

Enrichment refresh keeps existing positions and filters. A dismissible change
summary highlights added or updated entities and relationships. Evidence filters
select provider, source observation date, and relationship type; unmatched
relationships are hidden while entities remain visible.

Infrastructure summaries let you collapse or expand CIDR, IP, and Port types.
Save a named view to retain filters, positions, and display mode. Named views are
personal to this account and sketch in this browser; they are not shared views
and do not transfer automatically to another browser.

## Concurrent editing and setup

Entity and analysis saves check the version you originally read. If another
editor or enrichment changed the entity, your draft is retained and the save is
rejected. Copy your draft before explicitly loading the latest version, then
reconcile and save. Case-item edits also reject stale versions.

Profile → **Setup diagnostics** checks service availability, database migration
revision, API/worker connector inventories, and matching build revisions. See
[local deployment](../developers/local-deployment.mdx) for matching images,
upgrades, and backup/restore procedures.
